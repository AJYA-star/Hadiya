These terms apply when you use Hadiya, as a customer or as a shop owner. By creating an account or placing an order you agree to them.

2. What Hadiya is

Hadiya is a marketplace platform. It connects customers with independent gift wrapping shops. Hadiya is not itself a wrapping shop. The wrapping, collection and delivery are provided by the shop you choose, and the shop is responsible for them.

3. Accounts

4. Ordering as a customer

  • Prices, services and opening hours are set by each shop and shown before you order.
  • The total shown includes the service price and the delivery fee. You can choose to drop the gift off yourself where the shop offers it.
  • A shop may accept or reject an order. You are told of the decision in the app.
  • Please do not hand over items that are illegal, dangerous or of very high value without agreeing special arrangements with the shop first.

5. Payment

Orders are paid online through the payment method offered at checkout. Hadiya passes the shop its share after deducting its commission. Online payment will be switched on only when it is ready, and the app will say so.

6. Cancellations and refunds

You may ask to cancel before the shop starts work. Once wrapping has started the shop may refuse a refund for the service. If a shop does not deliver what was agreed, or damages your gift through its fault, contact us and we will help to resolve it with the shop. Refund details will be confirmed when online payment starts.

7. Shops

  • Every shop is reviewed and approved by Hadiya before it appears to customers. Approval is not a guarantee of quality.
  • Shops must give honest descriptions and prices, and handle customers’ gifts with care.
  • Shops pay Hadiya a commission on the service price of each completed order. The rate is shown to the shop and may change with notice. The delivery fee belongs to the shop.
  • Shops are independent businesses and are responsible for their own licences, taxes and staff.
  • We may suspend or remove a shop that breaks these terms or harms customers.

8. Acceptable use

Do not misuse the app, post false or abusive content, try to break into accounts or the system, or use Hadiya for anything unlawful.

9. Liability

To the extent allowed by law, Hadiya is not liable for loss or damage to a gift that happens while it is with a shop, or for delays caused by a shop. Hadiya’s total responsibility to you for an order is limited to the amount you paid for that order. Nothing here limits rights you cannot lose under UAE law.

10. Changes and law

We may update these terms and will change the date above. The laws of the United Arab Emirates apply, and disputes go to the competent courts of the Emirate where Hadiya is licensed.
